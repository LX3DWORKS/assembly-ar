Camera access

Assembly AR needs your Android camera to track the real-world position of your CAD assembly as you move around it.

Android will ask for permission after you tap the button.

Camera access can also be requested by Start AR. If your browser blocked access, use Chrome site settings to allow it. HTTPS and an ARCore-compatible Android device are required.

Components

Anchor uses detected surfaces/features, NOT recognition of the exact CAD part. Keep the physical assembly stationary, move the phone slowly, and use visually rich surroundings. Alignment is approximate and cannot certify weld or assembly conformity.

Use HTTPS and Chrome on an ARCore-capable Android phone. A stored calibration only restores model offsets; the spatial anchor must be recreated each session. Internet is needed to load the CAD and AR libraries.
