Recognition workflow

1. Rotate CAD in preview to match the physical viewing angle. 2. Start AR in Depth mode, aim at the product, choose Place roughly. 3. Scan from a few angles and press Find assembly. This matches measured 3D surfaces against the STEP model, not visual image recognition.

Inspection notes
Depth distance is only an experimental screening estimate. Unobserved components cannot be approved automatically. A 5 mm threshold is not a verified 5 mm measurement accuracy.

WebXR AR requires Chrome on ARCore-compatible Android through HTTPS. STEP geometry is processed locally in the browser, with online libraries. Generic shapes, repetitive geometry and shiny metal can produce wrong matches. The physical assembly must remain stationary.
